# Trademark policy

The BSD licence covers the Phoxtail **code**. It does not grant permission to
use the Phoxtail **name, wordmark or logo**. This document sets out the use that
is permitted anyway.

The licence lets you copy, modify and sell the software. This policy is about
what you may call the result.

## Permitted without asking

- Saying that your product, service or site is built with, runs on, or is
  compatible with Phoxtail.
- Listing Phoxtail among the technologies or services you work with, and using
  the logo to do so — linking to <https://phoxtail.com> where you can.
- Redistributing Phoxtail unmodified, under its own name, with the marks intact.
- Using the name and logo in articles, talks, tutorials and course material.

## Not permitted without written permission

- Naming a company, product or package so that it appears official —
  `Phoxtail Enterprise`, `Phoxtail Cloud`, `phoxtail-pro`. A prefix or suffix is
  fine for an open-source project that does not imply endorsement, on the
  pattern `phoxtail-foo`.
- Using the logo as your own mark, or altering it and still calling it the
  Phoxtail logo.
- Registering the name, or a confusingly similar one, as a company name.

## Forks

You may fork Phoxtail — that is what the licence is for. A fork you modify and
distribute must carry its own name and its own logo. It may say it is derived
from Phoxtail.

## Asking

Anything not covered here, or a use you think should be permitted: email
<info@phoxtail.com>. Permission is usually a formality.
