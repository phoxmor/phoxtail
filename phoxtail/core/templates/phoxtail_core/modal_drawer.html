{% comment %} A drawer for the core modals, at either level (modal.html, modal_level_1.html: blank canvases, each showing whatever its content view returns). A view that wants one extends this and fills its blocks: the title (block title) with the close button beside it, the body (block body), which scrolls, and the footer: Close, then the drawer's own actions (block actions), or a footer of its own (block footer). It slides in from the right, 480px wide (block width), standing on the ground, so what sits in it rises. The modal around it is the dialog, named by the drawer's title (modal.js). Closing it (the close button, Close, a click outside) closes that modal, whichever level it is on. {% endcomment %} {% load i18n phoxtail_design_tags %}
