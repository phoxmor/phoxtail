Secondary and danger buttons on the account pages are now one button that
differs only in label colour, and every action row stacks full width instead of
going side by side on wide screens. "Send me a sign-in code" on the login page
is now a secondary button, so only Login reads as the primary choice.

The inactive-account page offers a Go back to homepage button rather than a
bare link, and the login page's passkey and sign-in-code alternatives now share
one action stack with Login instead of sitting behind a divider.
