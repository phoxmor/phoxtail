Indicators that show a state and never act: `phx-badge` (a count in the brand
colour, sitting on a button's corner), `phx-tag` (a status in a word) and
`phx-notice` (a status in a sentence, with an icon and an optional
`phx-notice__title`). Tags and notices take a tone, `--success`, `--warning`,
`--destructive` or `--info`, read from the colour family of that name (core's
default, or a site palette role named so), and are neutral grey without one. A
tag laid over other content (a card's corner) adds `phx-tag--overlay`, so
nothing shows through it.
