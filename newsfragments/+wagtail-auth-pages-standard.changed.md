The login for private pages and the prompt for password-protected pages now
look like the rest of the authentication pages: the same layout, embers and
card, the shared form fields with their password visibility toggle, and the
standard button. The login offers a Forgot your password? link, and all of
their text can be translated. The password prompt no longer tells logged-in
visitors their account needs more access, since a page password does not
depend on the account.
