The homepage of a freshly hatched project is refreshed. Its cards, version
badge and Open dashboard button share one frosted glass, the top bar
lines up with the cards, and the embers match the authentication pages.

The phoenix keeps its design with one light shaping every part, wings that
rest on the shell, a crest that grows from behind the crown and a shadow on
the ground as it floats. Its eyes now close in place when it blinks and its
pupils stay inside the eyes. When reduced motion is asked for, it no longer
floats or flutters and the embers are left out.
