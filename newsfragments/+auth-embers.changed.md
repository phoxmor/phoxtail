The authentication pages, the private-page login and the page password prompt now
show the hatchling's rising embers behind the card, in place of the static
orbs and grid. The embers move at the same pace on any screen and refresh
rate, react only to a mouse, and are left out when reduced motion is asked
for.

In light mode the card is now a bright glass that reads above the page while
the embers still glimmer through it, and its shadow is tinted by the surface,
with a faint halo that keeps the top edge visible. The card no longer changes
on hover.
