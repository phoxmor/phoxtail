A `phoxtail_core/modal_drawer.html` template gives the core modals a ready
drawer, at either level: a content view extends it and fills its title, body
and actions (Close comes first in the footer), or replaces the whole footer,
and may set its width (480px by default). Its close button, Close and a click
outside close the modal it is in. The modals themselves stay blank canvases
for any other content.
