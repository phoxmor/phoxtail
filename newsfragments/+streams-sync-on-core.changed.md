The Streams Sync admin uses core's list page, cards, search, paginator and
drawer: a grey page with its icon before its title, the search on top with
Remote/Local beside the remote picker, and each variant a card with its
picture and its state in the corner. A variant's drawer is titled by its name
and shows, line by line in titled sections, its own fields and its block's,
how it stands against the remote in words, and its previews by device and
theme, a tap opening the picture large. A pull or push closes the drawer and
the list redraws itself on the page it was on; a refused one keeps the drawer
open and says why. The view's own 2,300-line stylesheet is gone.
