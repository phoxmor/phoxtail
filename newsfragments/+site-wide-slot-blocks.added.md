Shared blocks can now be pinned to a site-wide slot (`Block.site_slot`: head/body anchors in the page template) and render automatically on every page of a site that fills in their shared content — no per-page placement needed; combined with page-type restrictions it scopes to every page of those types. A page that places the block itself takes over (its own position and variant, or a "hide on this page" toggle), variants cascade page → site (`SharedBlock.variant`) → block default, missing locales fall back to the default locale's content, and `Block.render_in_preview` keeps tracking scripts out of previews and screenshots. The streams v1 API, the MCP studio tools, and the cross-project push envelope all carry the new fields.
