`.phx-button` is the one button: `--primary` (the brand colour, white text, the
one step that finishes a drawer or form), `--secondary` (every other action, its
fill from the box it sits in), `--danger`, `--full` for a full-width row, and
`--small` for actions repeated on every row of a list. A button marked
`aria-current` (the page you are on) wears the brand colour; a disabled one
fades to 0.38 and ignores hover and press.
