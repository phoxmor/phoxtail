`.phx-button` is the one button: `--primary` (the brand colour, white text, the
one step that finishes a drawer or form), `--secondary` (every other action, its
fill from the box it sits in), `--danger`, and `--full` for a full-width row. A
button marked `aria-current` (the page you are on) wears the brand colour; a
disabled one fades to 0.38 and ignores hover and press.
