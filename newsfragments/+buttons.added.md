`.phx-button` is the one button: `--primary` (the brand colour, white text, the
one step that finishes a drawer or form), `--secondary` (every other action, its
fill from the box it sits in), `--danger` (filled red, white text, for a step
that removes something), `--full` for a full-width row, and three sizes around
the default: `--xsmall` for actions repeated on every row of a list, `--small`
for an action beside other content, and `--large` for the one action a page
leads to. A button marked `aria-current` (the page you are on) wears the brand
colour; a disabled one fades to 0.38 and ignores hover and press.
