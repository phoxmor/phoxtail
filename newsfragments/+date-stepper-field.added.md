A `date_stepper` field draws `‹ date ›` for a page that shows one day or week at
a time: arrows step to the neighbouring day or week, a click anywhere on the
date opens the browser's calendar, and a typed date is sent on Enter. `text`
shows the day or week in the page's language in place of the date; `period="week"`
keeps the arrows still while a week's text changes width. The pill
takes its fill and shadow from the box it sits in.
