The chatbot now acts as the person chatting. Each turn mints a short-lived token owned by that person, scoped to their own permissions and revoked when the turn ends, and the tools offered are the ones that token covers. Two consequences: every tool call is attributed to the person who asked for it rather than to whoever ran `phoxtail auth login` in the container, and the chatbot's tool list — which had silently collapsed to the handful of tools that name no permission, because a caller with no credential is refused by every door that asks for one — is restored, per person.
